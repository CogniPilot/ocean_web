Ryzen 9 5950X and RTX 3090. Rumoca uses a uniform 144 × 64 × 64 lattice with 16 cells per diameter. It ran on 10 CPU threads or the GPU. OpenFOAM refines its 52,953-cell mesh near the rotor and blade walls. It ran on four CPU ranks or one rank with GPU velocity and turbulence solves.

Build times cover the Rumoca native executable, or 2.66 s for source generation plus 66.02 s for a cold OpenCL build. OpenFOAM used the v2412 binary. Its GPU build time covers the adapter compilation. Solve times exclude visualization export.

*Another job shared the GPU during this interval. For the same later 60 steps with that job stopped, four CPU ranks took 12 s, one CPU rank took 54 s, and the GPU-assisted run took 53 s.
