# Red Sea shipwreck scans

The Yatush and Satil mobile assets are adaptations of photogrammetric surveys by the University of Haifa's VISEAON – Treibitz Marine Imaging Lab. The Yatush model also credits Dmitry Churilov, IUI Eilat. Both source models are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- [Yatush source model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/yatush-shipwreck-325fa7ccf92b40a083a39ca4c6072b86)
- [Satil source model](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/satil-shipwreck-00c31066177e4e8ab9ebdc9cd4b2b8e2)
- [Original OBJ archives and textures](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10988972)

Matan Yuval and Tali Treibitz (2024). “Releasing a dataset of 3D models of artificial reefs from the northern red-sea for 3D printing and virtual reality applications.” *Remote Sensing Applications: Society and Environment*, 36, 101305. [Published paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rsase.2024.101305)

Ocean Web processed the meshes for mobile display, including removal of detached fragments and the seabed apron, geometric simplification and conversion to glTF. The lower hull is reconstructed where the original surveys have no underside imagery. Each site has a separate simplified collision surface. The game project’s code license applies separately from these assets.
